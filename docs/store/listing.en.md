## Full description (max 4000)
Focus Tycoon turns your study, work or reading time into a small isometric zoo that grows at your own pace.

**How it works**
Pick a focus session, put your phone aside and come back when it ends. Every completed minute earns coins, nature points and experience to plant trees, lay paths, build habitats and adopt animals.

**Focus your way**
- 15, 25, 45, 50 or 90 minute sessions, or any length from 5 to 180.
- Study, work, reading and project modes.
- Cycles with scheduled breaks.
- Optional background sounds: rain, forest or soft noise.
- Daily and weekly stats by mode.

**Breathing breaks**
Guided breathing (box 4-4, 4-7-8, coherent 5-5) and 3, 5 or 10 minute mindfulness sessions, with an animation that sets the pace.

**Your zoo**
- A 24×24 tile map you expand step by step.
- Seventeen species across four biomes: rabbit, beaver, hedgehog and duck in the meadow; penguin, seal and puffin on the ice; koala, fox, squirrel and owl in the forest; elephant, zebra, flamingo, lion, hippo and rhino on the savanna.
- Trees, flowers, shrubs, paths, decorations and habitats for every biome.
- Fifteen levels with new items to unlock.

**No guilt**
If you stop a session early, it still counts the minutes you did. Skip a day and you lose nothing. Breaks are part of the habit.

**Private by default**
Your progress stays on your device. No ads, no trackers and no email accounts: only an anonymous backup of your resources.

**Optional purchases**
The whole game is free to play. Premium is a one-time purchase with extra sounds, extended stats and exclusive decorations. It never sells resources or speeds up progress.

Focus Tycoon is a timer with rewards: it does not measure or verify real concentration, and its breathing exercises are not a substitute for any treatment.
