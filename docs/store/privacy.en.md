## Summary
Focus Tycoon does not ask for your name, email, location or contacts. It shows no ads and uses no analytics or tracking tools. Almost everything is stored only on your device. The only thing that leaves your device is an anonymous backup of your in-game resources.

## Data stored on your device
- Your save: park, animals, resources, level, achievements and settings.
- Your focus and meditation history: length, mode, start and end time, and whether each session was completed or stopped early.
- An anonymous session token used for sync.

This data stays on your device, except for what the next section describes. The app turns off Android automatic backup, so uninstalling it deletes everything.

## Data sent to our server
To back up your resources and prevent cheating, the app talks to our own server hosted on Cloudflare:
- **Anonymous player ID:** created at random on first launch. It is not linked to your name, email or Google account.
- **Resource changes:** each change in coins, nature points and experience, with its reason (for example "session completed" or "item bought") and time.
- **Resource balance:** your current coins, nature and experience.

Your detailed session history and the contents of your park are **not** sent.

## IP address and technical logs
Like any internet service, the server receives your IP address. We use it only in memory to limit abuse (requests per minute and new player sign-ups). We do not store it in the database. Technical error logs contain a request ID and an error code, not your IP or personal data.

## Purchases
In-app purchases are processed by Google Play. We do not receive or store your payment details. The app only receives confirmation of which product you bought so it can unlock it on your device. [Google's privacy policy](https://policies.google.com/privacy) applies.

## Notifications
Reminders are off by default. If you turn them on, they are scheduled on your device. We do not send server push notifications.

## How we use data
- To run the game and keep your progress.
- To back up your resources and detect impossible or duplicate changes.
- To protect the service against abuse.

We do not sell or share your data with third parties, and we do not use it for advertising.

## Service providers
- **Cloudflare, Inc.:** hosts the server and database.
- **Google LLC:** processes payments through Google Play.

## Retention
Server data is kept while your player ID exists. If there is no activity for 24 months, we may delete it.

## Your rights and how to delete your data
You can ask to access, correct or delete your server data by writing to [contact email]. Because we do not know who you are, we need your player ID to find your data. It is shown under Profile › Settings. [To do: show the ID and add a "Delete my server data" button before release.] We reply within 30 days.

## Children
The game is suitable for all ages and does not knowingly collect personal data. If you are a parent or guardian and want data deleted, write to us.

## Security
All traffic uses HTTPS. On the server, the token is stored only as a hash.

## Disclaimer
Focus Tycoon is a timer with rewards. It does not measure or verify real concentration. The breathing and mindfulness exercises are not a health service and are not a substitute for any treatment.

## Changes
If we change this policy, we update the date above and tell you in the app when the change is significant.
